This is a 24hr analog clock. It's consists of a clockface with hourlines, an hour triangle, minute ticks, a minute dot, and a second marker.
Start on the `EAST` side, `NORTH` is 1/4 of the way around the clock, and the `WEST` is 1/2 way around the clock.
At midnight, there are no hourlines. The first hourline appears near the east end of the clock, at 1am. At 12pm, the first half of the day fills the first half of the hexagon.
On the perimeter of the hexagon, a line is drawn longer with each second.
The current minute is represented by a dot at the end of the counter-clockwise ticks.
As the hours pass, a new line is drawn inside of the hexagon. The first 4 hour lines appear on the upper-right segment of the clock. The next 4 appear on the upper-center segment of the clock. At noon, the first half of the day is displayed on the clock.
